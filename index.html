About Cinci

Where beauty meets care

Cinci Beauty Spa is a calm, private studio on the third floor of Premium Towers in Pretoria Central. We specialise in beauty, cosmetic and personal care: poly gel nails, pedicures, lash extensions, waxing, facials, dermaplaning, massage and wood therapy.

Every visit is by appointment, so you get our full attention, premium products and high hygiene standards. Leave feeling renewed, confident and glowing.
